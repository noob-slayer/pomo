Why a timer helps you study

Open-ended studying is easy to put off and hard to sustain. A timer turns "study biology" into a concrete 25- or 40-minute block with a break waiting on the other side. You start sooner, take real breaks, and — because pomo logs every session — you can actually see the hours add up before an exam.

Made for study sessions

  • Any session length — 25/40/50-minute blocks or a custom timer.
  • Track subjects as tasks and see time logged per subject.
  • History — review your study hours by day and week.
  • Study rooms — study together and see who's focusing right now.
  • Offline & installable — works in the library with flaky wifi.

Frequently asked questions

How long should a study block be?

Start with 25 minutes and a 5-minute break; increase to 40–50 minutes for deep subjects. pomo lets you set any length.

Can I study with classmates?

Yes — create a room, share the link, and study together with a live view of who's focusing.
