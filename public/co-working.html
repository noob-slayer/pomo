What is body doubling?

Body doubling is the simple trick of working next to someone else so their presence helps you start — and keep going. It's a favourite for studying, deep work, and staying focused with ADHD. pomo brings it online: no video call to set up, just a shared timer and a live view of who's focusing.

How co-working lobbies work

  • Create a lobby and share the link over WhatsApp or anywhere.
  • Individual mode — everyone runs their own timer; a live roster shows who's focusing, on a break, or offline.
  • Sync mode — one shared clock keeps the whole group in lockstep.
  • Team stats & leaderboards — see focused minutes across the group, plus friendly challenges.
  • Live chat & kudos — cheer each other on between sessions.
