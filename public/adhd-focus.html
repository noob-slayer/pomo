Body doubling, online

Body doubling is the simple idea that it's easier to start and stay on task when someone else is present and working too. Lots of people with ADHD lean on it. pomo brings it online: create a room, share the link, and a live roster shows who's focusing right now — the quiet accountability of working "next to" someone, without a video call.

Structure that keeps tasks small

  • Short timed blocks so a task is "just 25 minutes," not open-ended.
  • Live co-working rooms — see who's focusing and start together.
  • One task at a time — pick what you're on and press start.
  • History & streaks for a sense of momentum.
  • Playful backgrounds & ambient sound to make focus time inviting.

Frequently asked questions

Why does working alongside others help?

The presence of someone else focusing provides gentle external structure that can make starting — often the hardest step — feel easier.

Do I need to talk to anyone?

No. Rooms are silent by default — you just share the space. There's optional chat and kudos if you want them.

pomo is a productivity tool, not a medical device, and isn't a substitute for professional advice or treatment.
