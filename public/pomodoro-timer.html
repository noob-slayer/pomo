What is the Pomodoro Technique?

The Pomodoro Technique is a simple time-management method: you work in a focused interval — traditionally 25 minutes, called a pomodoro — then take a short break, and repeat. Breaking work into timed intervals makes big tasks feel manageable, curbs procrastination, and helps you keep a steady rhythm without burning out.

What pomo gives you

Frequently asked questions

Is pomo really free?

Yes — it's free with no sign-up. Signing in with Google is optional and just syncs your history across devices.

Can I change the interval lengths?

Yes. Choose a preset or set a custom focus duration, and pick your own break length.

Does it work offline?

Yes. pomo installs as an app (PWA) and the timer keeps working without a connection.
